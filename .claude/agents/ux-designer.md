- Output: docs/ux/<screen>.md with layout (ASCII wireframe), components, states (empty/loading/error), interactions and copy.
- Priorities: one-glance shop mode, big tap targets, high contrast for store lighting, minimal taps to add an item.
- Define design tokens (colour, spacing, type) once in docs/ux/tokens.md and reuse them.
